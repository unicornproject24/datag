## 🎯 Design Elements

### Typography
- **Headings**: 4xl-5xl for page titles
- **Post Titles**: 2xl for featured, lg for grid
- **Body Text**: Base size, relaxed leading
- **Metadata**: xs-sm, gray-500

### Colors
- **Background**: gray-50 (light gray)
- **Cards**: white with border
- **Primary**: Accent color for CTAs
- **Text**: gray-900 (headings), gray-600 (body)

### Spacing
- **Container**: max-w-6xl
- **Padding**: py-12 to py-16
- **Grid Gaps**: gap-6 to gap-12
- **Card Padding**: p-6 to p-8

### Images
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (aspect-video)
- **Hover Effect**: scale-105 transform
- **Loading**: Object-cover for proper fit
