1. **Bookmark the admin panel** - You'll use it often
   - `your-site.com/#admin`

2. **Keep admin URL private** - Don't share publicly until you add authentication

3. **Start with demo content** - Initialize the database, then replace content gradually

4. **Test on mobile** - Your site is responsive, but always verify

5. **Save your work** - Always click "Save" in admin panel before closing

6. **Take it step by step** - Deploy first, customize later
